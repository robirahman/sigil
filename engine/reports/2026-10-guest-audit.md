# Surprise audit of Robi's guest-vs-AI games (2026-10-06/07)

Branch `audit-guest-2026-10`. Firebase `completed_games` since 2026-10-06, downloaded read-only
(`eval_games.py download` / `hydrate`): **24 games**, all the competitive variant, all against the Rust
Hard tier (`__ai_rust_hard__`, 10 s/move in the browser), no clock.

**Record.** The AI won 3 and lost 5 under v23 (Oct 6), and won 9 and lost 7 under v25 (Oct 7, 01:48-06:13 UTC).
Nine of the 12 wins ended by the human resigning (`endReason: forfeit`). These are real wins: Robi
resigned lost positions after the AI got the advantage. The 01:48 game may still have run v23 from
the service-worker cache; the v23 build audited it too (below).

## Method

`surprise_audit.py` pipeline (depth-4 eval of every position -> flag -> probe -> report), for both
opponent-turn falls (`--side opp`: the human found a reply the engine did not expect) and own-turn
falls (`--side own`: the AI's own move walked into trouble), plus `final_blow_probe.py`.
Threshold 1.0 stone; a case is CONFIRMED when the depth-6 value of the resulting position agrees.

Each game was audited with the engine that played it and with today's v27:

New tooling (commit 62c2e30d) so that old releases are audited as they shipped:
* `SIGIL_AUDIT_ENGINE=shipped` makes `eval_games` / `surprise_audit` / `final_blow_probe` search with
  the build's own `SHIPPED_EVAL` and `SHIPPED_POLICY`. Before this, every audit searched with `tfit`
  and the policy off, i.e. the v23 engine, whatever build was loaded.
* The probe also records the reply's rank in the POLICY stream (`policy_rank`) when the policy is on.
* `runner_surprise.sh` / `launch_surprise.sh`: `ENGINE_REF` builds the engine from a tag or branch while
  the harness comes from `BRANCH`. `MODE=guest` runs eval once, then opp and own audits and final_blow.
* `eval_games.py hydrate` no longer skips Providence banks (`pm:`) and Fissure walls (`x`). The Rust
  engine plays them since v22; 4 of the 24 games were being dropped.

Note on depth: the browser's Hard tier searches 10 s per move in wasm. "Sees at d5/d6" below means a
deeper search than the depth-4 audit baseline finds the reply; whether the browser reached that depth
in that position is not known from the records.

## Patterns

1. **Losses end in one tactical spell blow after a slow drift.** 10 of 12 losses have a MATE-band
   swing, usually after 5-15 turns at -1 to -2. The blows are casts and dash+casts: Harvest (3 times,
   all v25 games with Gather/Harvest boards), Scatter, Syzygy, Fury, Starfall, Corrupt.
2. **Generator gaps remain the main blind spot, even for v27.**
   * 5 of the 9 decisive human replies were never generated by the playing build's stream.
   * v27's policy stream still does not generate 5 of its 6 remaining confirmed MATE-band cases within
     its cap: Scatter, Fury, Syzygy dash+cast, Corrupt dash, and one not in the enumeration. A deeper
     search still finds the value of most of them through other lines.
   * The one v27 cannot see even at depth 6 is Syzygy dash+cast (04:45 t31).
3. **Horizon.** 2 decisive replies were predicted at the root but their consequence was beyond depth 6
   (02:10 t19), or beyond d5 (06:13 t25). v27 is not surprised by either at depth 4, so the better
   eval (v26's `nnue_spell3`) prices them.
4. **Own-move blunders from level positions.** Five of six are avoided by a depth-5/6 search, so the
   browser's search depth at 10 s matters. Two are dashes (06:13 t22 and t24); one stays blind at d6.
5. **Opening.** In the same positions, v27's no-book opening differs from the book's sigil in 21 of 24
   games. It prefers the charms (b7 Splash/Slash/Sprout, a7/c7 Charge) and the Hurricane/Bewitch rituals.
   * The three v25 losses with a Gather opening (02:03, 02:10, 02:15) are the three Harvest-blow games.
   * The two c7 Charge openings (04:48, 04:54) were quick wins.
   * 24 games cannot separate this from chance.
6. **How the wins were built.** Mostly slow accumulation: +1 by turns 10-20 and +2 near the end, then a
   resignation (9 of 12) or a forced win the search found (+20 at d4: 00:35, 00:40, 04:47, 02:20, 03:09).
   Two quick wins (04:48, 04:54) went +1 from the second ply after a Charge opening. 05:03 was a see-saw
   with 9 small surprises; it was resigned with the AI's own d4 value at -1.

## Recommendations for the next training round

1. **Generator coverage for spell blows.**
   * Add `ai/data/benchmarks/guest_2026-10_cases.json` to the gates.
   * Raise the exploration share (step 3.2) for casts and dash+casts with non-default sacrifice pairs.
   * Weight those choice points in the next policy retrain. Measure the policy-stream rank of the Harvest,
     Scatter, Fury, Syzygy and Corrupt blows above (all currently not generated).
2. **Depth in the browser.** Most own-move blunders and two decisive replies need d5-d6.
   * Measure the depth the wasm Hard tier actually reaches at 10 s in these positions.
   * Weigh wasm threads (option B) or a node-rate pass against that.
3. **Competitive midgame eval.** The long -1/-2 drifts before the blow are positional. Use more
   competitive-variant self-play in the next data round; human starts are already half the starts.
4. **Opening.** Keep v27's book-off default and re-check the Gather/Harvest boards in the next guest games.
