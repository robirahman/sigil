# Step 2b: tfit_spell refitted on the Step 3 v2 data (2026-10-06)

Branch `train-s2b-refit`, cut from `train-s2-spell-eval`. Head before this report: 7a6a70f8.

## What was done

`fit_spell_eval_v2.py` refits the Step 2 per-spell table on the Step 3 v23 self-play data at
`gs://focus-surfer-494820-g0-sigil/data/s3/v2_2026-10-06/`. It uses the same method as Step 2:
a score fit, regularised towards the shared `sigil_stone` / `sigil_charged` weights, with
spell-held-out folds and a sign check against the outcome fit. The fit ran on one VM, run
`20261006T155615Z` (`live/fit_spell_v2.txt`).

* **Data:** 9,551,492 d4 positions (52,069 chunks, deduplicated from 104,026) and 828,881 d5
  positions. No chunk was unreadable. By game start: 4.75M fresh draws, 1.62M competitive and
  3.18M from human-game starts.
* **Split:** train 7,634,330 / test 1,917,162, split by game.
* **Check:** `check_exact` gave 0 mismatches over 600 positions (Python features against Rust
  `evaluate`). corr(tfit, d4 score) = 0.915.
* **Result:** preset `tfit_spell_v2` (table `SPELL_SIGIL_FIT_V2`) has tfit_spell's shape: one
  96 cs budget, table x3. The evaluate == dot test now also covers draws holding expansion
  spells.

## Offline (held-out test games; lower is better)

| preset | outcome LL (all) | score xent (all) | outcome LL (expansion draws, n=489k) | outcome LL (core-only draws, n=1.43M) |

| tfit | 0.63462 | 0.65184 | 0.65542 | 0.62748 |

| tfit_spell_v2 | **0.63361** | **0.65171** | 0.65563 | **0.62605** |

On d5 labels, tfit_spell_v2 is again best on both metrics: outcome LL 0.62854 vs 0.62901
(tfit_spell) and 0.62985 (tfit).

On expansion draws, tfit_spell (whose fallback is the shared weight) is slightly worse than tfit.
tfit_spell_v2 recovers part of that (0.65563 vs 0.65595) but does not reach tfit (0.65542).

Spell-held-out folds: the score fit beats the base on score xent in all four folds, including
the fold that holds out all six expansion spells (0.65698 vs 0.65716). Two terms disagree in
sign with the outcome fit at |delta| >= 0.5 cs: Meteor stone (+1.39 vs -0.73) and Storm Front
stone (+0.69 vs -0.34). Both are small.

## Conclusion

No measured difference. The refit is a slight offline improvement, worth about a third of
tfit_spell's own offline gain over tfit. It is flat in play at 10 s, including on draws holding
the new spells. The Step 2 table therefore generalises to the expansion spells through the
shared weights about as well as their own fitted rows do.

tfit_spell_v2 is equivalent in strength and has real weights for ids 39-44, so either can ship.
Prefer v2 if one table is to be maintained going forward, since it is fitted on v23 data with
the current rules.
