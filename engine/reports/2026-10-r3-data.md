# Round 3: v27 self-play data (competitive majority) and a blind-spot-weighted policy retrain (2026-10-07)

Branch `r3-data` (cut from `engine-improvement` cd38f524). Repeats round 2 (`2026-10-r2-data.md`) on the
shipped v27 engine, with two changes asked for by the guest audit (`2026-10-guest-audit.md`) and by Robi:

* the competitive variant is now a strict majority of the self-play data;
* the policy retrain puts extra loss weight on casts and dash+casts, especially those with a
  non-default sacrifice/keep choice, which is the audit's blind spot.

Nothing is shipped. `engine/src/policy_weights.rs` and every Rust file are untouched, so no
`cargo test` or policy parity run was needed.

  `COMP_FRAC_DEFAULT = 0.67`, and the docstring states the rule: competitive must always be a
  strict majority, per Robi on 2026-10-07. Future rounds inherit this default.

## Part B: policy retrain

**Tooling** (`policy_train.py`, `guest_policy_rank.py`, `gcp/r3_policy.sh`):

* **Example tags.** `extract` tags every example with the following fields:
  * its turn class, read from the levels the walk reached: move-only, cast, dash or dash+cast;
  * `nondef`: the target is not option 0 at a dash (sacrifices, landing) or cast (keep, outcome) level. Option 0 is the shipped generator's own first choice.
  * `comp`: the variant;
  * `xb`, set by `--xbetter M`: the example is an exploration row (a turn the stream never emits, scored at D-1) that beat the position's depth-D score by more than M. Such a row is added as an extra target.
* **Loss weights in `train`.** The `--w-cast`, `--w-dashcast`, `--w-nondef` and `--w-xb` flags multiply
  each example's loss. `--comp-share S` then scales the non-competitive examples down so that the
  competitive share of the training weight is at least S.
* **Reports.** `train` reports held-out NLL and the target's top-1/top-3 rank per level. Each is given
  overall and for three slices: cast or dash+cast, cast with a non-default choice, and exploration-better.
  `gate0` reports stream coverage on the same slices.

**Run** `r3p-train-20261007a`: one Spot c3d-standard-90 in europe-west4-b, 55 min.

* **Training data:**
  * 8,550 v27 chunks: 450 from each of the 19 runs, taken while the fleet was still playing.
  * 3,000 v25 chunks.
  * 380 more v27 chunks were held out by file for `gate0`. A chunk holds whole games.
* **Extraction:** 1,986,759 examples and 6.16 M choice-point levels.
  * By class: 1,250,279 move-only, 342,132 cast, 190,355 dash and 203,993 dash+cast.
  * 363,754 examples have a non-default choice.
* **Exploration-better examples: 2,757.** That is about 0.2% of explored positions: the depth-4 search
  rarely leaves a better spell turn unemitted.
* **Arena positions** (`runs/*/data/arena_*.npz`) were not used. They store positions and scores
  but not the played turn, so they give no policy target without re-searching.

**Competitive share of the training mix.** By examples it is 55.4%; competitive opening placements give
no example. By loss weight it is 56.9%, raised to **60.0%** by `--comp-share 0.6`.

Two warm starts from the shipped (v26/v27 = `r2_warm_20261007`) weights, 12 epochs each:

* `r3_warm`: no class weights.
* `r3_wtd`: `--w-cast 2 --w-dashcast 3 --w-nondef 2 --w-xb 4`.

Both are in `engine/harness/policy_weights/`; the raw results are in `engine/reports/r3-data/`.

## Reading

* **Neither candidate is worth shipping.**
  * r3_warm is a wash. The shipped weights were already fitted to v25 self-play, and v27 plays much the same.
  * r3_wtd ranks spell turns higher, but it pays for that in quiet-move ordering and comes out
    slightly negative. The pooled estimate of -7.5 is not significant.
* **The ordering is not the lever for the audit's blind spot.** The decisive guest blows are absent from the
  generated candidate set. Any gain there has to come from generation (R3-GEN's knob) or from deeper
  search.
* **Format compatibility.** Both weight files keep the `policy_weights.rs` format: NF × PW float32,
  same feature layout. R3-GEN's merge leaves `policy.rs` features and `policy_example` unchanged, and
  `r3-data` now has `origin/engine-improvement` merged in.

**Stacked with R3-GEN's exploration tail.** This is the guest suite under `set_policy_explore` preset 3
(`3,64,128,8,48,512,16`, now on `engine-improvement`); `--explore` is new in `guest_policy_rank.py`.

|---|---|---|
| shipped | 14 / 17 / 19 / 21 / 22 / 25 | 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 6 / 8 |
| r3_warm | 14 / 16 / 20 / 21 / 22 / 25 | 1 / 1 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 8 |

With the wider candidate set, r3_wtd still gains nothing within the root widths the search uses. Its one
extra case only appears at w500. Neither candidate won its arena, so the stacked arena (candidate +
preset 3 against shipped policy + preset 3) was not run, as the coordinator's condition required.
* **What to use the v27 dataset for.** It is competitive-majority and labelled by the shipped
  nnue_spell3 engine, so its main use is the next eval-network fit, not the policy.

## Notes for next time

* **Slow chunk download on the training VM.** `gcloud storage cp -I` took 28 minutes for 11,500
  chunks across regions. Run the training VM in the region the chunks came from, or tar the chunks per run.
* **Train while the fleet plays.** The training job ran on chunks from the fleet's first hour. With
  1.99 M examples the fit is not data-limited: r3_warm moved held-out NLL by only 0.005. That makes
  training early safe, and the policy and arena verdict could finish before the data run did.
* **Arena positions give no policy target.** If they are to feed the policy, `ab_search.py` should save the played turn (packed) next to each position.
