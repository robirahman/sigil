# Step 5: a small spell-conditioned network eval (2026-10-06)

Branch `train-s5-nneval`. Plan section: "Step 5" of `2026-10-training-plan.md`.
Architecture, quantisation and training target: `engine/harness/nn_eval.py` (docstring)
and `engine/src/nn.rs`.

## What was built

* `nnue_spell` = `tfit_spell` + clamp(net, +-96 cs). The network reads 78 occupancy bits
  plus the side. Spell conditioning is folded into the first layer once per draw:
  base row + spell-on-sigil embedding + place-in-sigil. After that comes a 128-wide
  accumulator, then 32 and 32 hidden units. Weights are int16/int8; the arithmetic is
  integer-only.
* `nets/nnue_spell.bin` (245 KB) is baked into the binary with `include_bytes!`.
  `nets/nnue_spell_golden.txt` holds 264 vectors written by the Python quantised
  forward pass:
  * `nn_matches_the_python_reference` pins Python == native.
  * `tools/nn-wasm-parity.js` pins wasm == the same vectors, through the
    `nn_eval_raw` export. 264/264 match on the plain build and on the `+simd128`
    build.
* The preset is off by default. Shipped defaults, the wasm version and `docs/sw.js`
  are unchanged.

## Training

Run `gs://…-sigil/runs/20261006T183349Z-sigil-s5-train/` on one Spot A100
(a2-highgpu-1g, europe-west4-a):
* Data: the Step 3 v2 depth-4 data, 19.1M positions; the game split trains on 14.9M
  and tests on 4.2M from 225k held-out games.
* Target: t = lam * sigmoid(k * score) + (1 - lam) * outcome.
* lam chosen on held-out outcome log-loss: **0.25** (`BEST.txt`).

Held-out results (outcome log-loss = nats per position against the game result;
score xent = cross-entropy against the depth-4 search score):

| split | tfit | tfit_spell | tfit_spell2 | **net (quantised)** | net score xent vs tfit_spell | at cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| game (80/20 by game) | 0.6333 | 0.6327 | 0.6309 | **0.5917** | 0.6126 vs 0.6117 | 30% |
| spell fold 0 | 0.6318 | 0.6312 | 0.6292 | **0.6061** | 0.6190 vs 0.6109 | 37% |
| spell fold 1 | 0.6310 | 0.6303 | 0.6284 | **0.6082** | 0.6223 vs 0.6140 | 37% |
| spell fold 2 | 0.6313 | 0.6306 | 0.6287 | **0.6094** | 0.6257 vs 0.6161 | 41% |

* **Offline gate: PASS.** On outcome log-loss the net beats `tfit_spell` by 0.041
  nats on the game split and 0.021-0.025 on the spell-held-out folds. For scale,
  `tfit`'s whole gain over material was 0.0017 and `tfit_spell`'s over `tfit` was
  about 0.002.
* h256 is no better (0.5921).

Caveats:
* **Score cross-entropy is slightly worse** than `tfit_spell`'s on every split. The
  net predicts game outcomes better than the depth-4 search does, but it does not
  imitate that search.
* **30-41% of outputs sit at the +-96 cs cap,** so the residual wants to be larger
  than the budget allows.
* **Quantised and float outputs differ by up to 69-80 cs on single positions.** On
  average, though, the quantised net's log-losses equal the float net's.

## Engine cost

The per-node cost is unchanged. `nn_bench.py` ran locally (Chromebook): depth 4 over
`positions_midgame.txt`, 2 interleaved rounds.

| eval | us/node (median) | ratio | nodes to depth 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| tfit_spell_v2 | 20.0 | 1.00 | 1,659,564 |
| nnue_spell | 19.9 | 0.99 | 2,012,216 (+21%) |

**Node-cost gate (< ~25%): PASS.** The per-draw fold is cached, and each leaf costs
one row add per occupied node plus two 32-wide layers. The leaf is a small part of a
node, which is mostly turn generation.

The tree to depth 4 is 21% larger, so time to depth rises by about as much. That is
a cost the arena prices in; the node-rate gate does not.

**Eval-pair smoke** (`gcp/smoke_eval_pair.py nnue_spell:tfit_spell_v2`, depth 4,
through `play_best`): 33/76 different turns and 64/76 different root scores. The
preset reaches play.

**Browser:** both wasm builds embed the 245 KB net. Gzip is the over-the-wire size.

| build | wasm size | gzip |
|---|---|---|
| shipped (v23) | 738 KB | 240 KB |
| nnue plain | 1,002 KB | — |
| nnue `+simd128` | 992 KB | 308 KB |

Parity holds on both builds. `+simd128` changes little here: LLVM auto-vectorises
only some of the int16 row adds.

## Arenas: nnue_spell vs tfit_spell_v2 (the shipped eval)

Fixed time, colour-swapped, `ab_search.py` knob `preset` (`eval=nnue_spell:tfit_spell_v2`),
on-demand c3d-highcpu-90 in europe-west4, 86 shards per VM, pooled from `GAME` lines with
`pool_shards.py`; no unfinished games; time matched (3.001 / 3.001 s per move).

| time | draws | runs | games | W-L | Elo (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 s | core packs | 20261006T205427Z-sigil-s5-a3a, 20261006T205500Z-sigil-s5-a3b | 3,439 | 2053-1386 | **+68.3 [+56.4, +80.1]** |
| 3 s | holding a Tectonic/Providence spell | 20261006T214056Z-sigil-s5-a3x | X3 | | X3E |
| 10 s | core packs | 20261006T214013Z-sigil-s5-a10a, 20261006T214030Z-sigil-s5-a10b | X10 | | X10E |

The network side searches about half a ply shallower in the same time (mean depth 5.81
vs 6.27 at 3 s: the tree to a given depth is ~20% larger) and still wins.

Split by draw at 3 s (`split_by_draw.py`, every spell 700-890 games): every spell's
interval excludes parity, from Decay +40 to Harvest +91 Elo; the intervals
(about +-25) overlap, so the gain is broad.
