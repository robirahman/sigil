## Part B: retrained generator policy

**Training** (`engine/gcp/r2_policy.sh`, run `r2p-train-20261007a`, one Spot VM): 8,000 v25 chunks
(10 depth-4 runs + 2 depth-5 runs) -> 1.42 M examples / 4.30 M choice-point levels via
`policy_train.py extract` (the Rust `policy_example` path). Trained from zero and warm-started from
the shipped weights (`--init compiled`, new), 12 epochs each. v2 (round 1) data was not used: the
v25 data is what the shipped engine now plays, and it alone is 10x the round-1 training set.

| weights | held-out NLL / level (game split) | gate0 w6 / w12 / w24 / w40 (100 held-out chunks, 5,953 positions) |
|---|---|---|
| shipped (`w1`) | 1.072 | 66.6 / 75.4 / 83.0 / 87.3 |
| retrained, zero init | 0.977 | 67.8 / 76.7 / 84.3 / 88.3 |
| **retrained, warm start** | **0.977** | **67.9 / 76.7 / 84.2 / 88.4** |

The big loss gain is mostly the first-step level (M: 1.81 -> 1.57); the shipped weights were fitted
to tfit-era choices. Coverage gains are ~1 point.

**Step 1 suites** (`bench_suites.py`, nnue_spell + policy 96, fixed nodes): neutral. Human finds
w6/w10 17.5/29.2 -> 18.3/30.0; surprise replies w10/w40 33.8/46.4 -> 36.6/49.2; sees-rate at
50k/300k/1.5M 45.1/58.7/76.3 -> 45.1/58.0/75.7 (one case = 0.3 points); final-blow unchanged.

**Node cost:** unchanged. The weights only reorder the same options; arena mean depth differs by
0.01-0.02 ply in every arena below.

## Notes for next time

* Spot c3d preemption hit 6 of 28 VMs in 3 h, mostly us-central1-a and europe-west1-b.
* `ab_search.py` A/Bs default to adaptive OFF and policy OFF (`SIGIL_AB_BASE=legacy`); release-gating
  arenas must set `SIGIL_AB_BASE=shipped`.
* The retrained weights' gain is small because the shipped policy already captures most of what
  the stream ordering can learn from depth-4 choices; a bigger lever is soft targets from the
  graded root candidates (the data has them) instead of the single chosen turn.
