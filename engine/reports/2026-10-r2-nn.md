# Round 2: network eval retrain (2026-10-07)

Branch `r2-nn`, cut from `providence-bank` at a3771ed0 (engine v25, shipped eval `nnue_spell` +
generator policy 96). Goal: fix the Step 5 network's known limits — output saturation at the
±96 cs cap, the older `tfit_spell` base, a slightly worse score fit than the hand eval, and a
large quantised-vs-float gap.

## What changed

* **Base `tfit_spell_v2`.** The new nets are residuals on v24's per-spell table:
  `nnue_spell2` / `nnue_spell3` = `TFIT_SPELL_V2` + clamp(net, ±cap).
* **Quantisation-aware training.** `nn_eval.py train --qat N` trains the last N epochs through
  `forward_q`, a fake-quant forward with straight-through gradients: weights on their integer
  grids, every hidden layer floored to 1/64, output floored to whole centistones. On 2,347
  positions it equals `qforward` (and therefore `src/nn.rs`) with 0 mismatches. So the net that
  ships is the net that was optimised. The reported "quant vs float max diff" stays 60-80 cs,
  but after QAT that compares against the non-quantised float path, which is no longer the
  model being trained; the quantised log-loss is the one that counts.
* **Cap 160 / 256** instead of 96.
* **Tooling.**
  * `runner_nn.sh` / `launch_nn.sh` take `EVALS` (the prep columns) and `JOBS_FILE` (one
    `tag args` training job per line), plus `JOBS=prep-only` for a wide CPU prep VM.
  * `ab_search.py SIGIL_AB_BASE=shipped` plays both arms from the shipped engine (adaptive
    widening + generator policy). Before it, every `preset` arena ran adaptive OFF and policy
    OFF, i.e. not the engine the site plays.
  * `nn::by_name`, the wasm export `nn_eval_raw_net`, and `tools/nn-wasm-parity.js NET`.

How to read the table:

* **Raising the cap fixes saturation** (33% at cap → 2%) and is worth 0.004-0.007 nats of
  outcome log-loss.
* **Hidden 256 does not help.**
* **λ trades outcome fit against score fit.** λ 0.25 is best on outcomes; λ 0.5 matches the
  search score as well as the shipped net does.
* **The retrain reproduces the shipped net** (0.5923 vs 0.5921).

Spell-held-out folds (train on draws without six spells, test on draws holding them), λ 0.25,

Every cap generalises to unseen spells about equally well (−0.021 to −0.022 vs the hand eval).
The shipped `nnue_spell` cannot be compared on these folds, because it was trained on their test

Two presets went forward to the arena:

* `nnue_spell2`: cap 256, λ 0.25, the best outcome fit.
* `nnue_spell3`: cap 256, λ 0.5, the best score fit.

## Parity and cost

* **Python == native:** 264 golden vectors each (`round2_networks_match_python_and_sit_on_tfit_spell_v2`).
* **Preset structure:** each preset equals `tfit_spell_v2` + net.
* **wasm:** parity 264/264 for nnue_spell, nnue_spell2 and nnue_spell3 (`tools/nn-wasm-parity.js <dir> <net>`).
* **Node cost:** `examples/bench.rs` at depth 4 with policy 96 over 76 midgame positions:

| eval | us/node | nodes to depth 4 |
|---|---|---|
| nnue_spell | 18.6 / 19.3 | 1,991,616 (hash 5a13846bc3228203 = v25) |
| nnue_spell2 | 17.7 / 18.7 | 2,343,457 (+18%) |
| nnue_spell3 | 18.4 | 2,195,283 (+10%) |

Per-node cost is unchanged. The trees are larger, which the fixed-time arenas price in.

* **wasm size:** baking all three nets makes it 1.60 MB. A release would bake only the winner
  (~245 KB per net).

The better offline outcome fit (`nnue_spell2`) does not play better. The net that also matches
the search score (`nnue_spell3`, λ 0.5) does. This repeats Step 2's lesson: a value fitted only
to outcomes is not what the search needs.

## Result

**`nnue_spell3` passes every gate:**

| gate | result |
|---|---|
| offline, game split | outcome log-loss 0.5880 vs 0.5921, score xent equal |
| parity | native == Python == wasm, including policy-ordered tree parity: `tools/policy-wasm-parity.js 3240153792d478e5 4 nnue_spell3` over 76 positions at depth 4 |
| node cost | no change |
| arenas vs the shipped `nnue_spell` on the shipped v25 search | +29.5 at 3 s; +40.1 (core) and +38.1 (expansion) at 10 s |

The cap-256 / λ 0.25 net (`nnue_spell2`) beats it offline on outcomes but not over the board.
