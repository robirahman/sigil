# Step 4: a learned policy inside the generator (2026-10-06)

Branch `train-s4-policy` (cut from `train-s3-data-v2`, with `train-s1-benchmarks` merged).
Plan section: "Step 4" of `2026-10-training-plan.md`.

## What was built

* `engine/src/policy.rs`: a log-linear model at every choice point of the turn generator
  (first step, stop/continue, which cast or the dash, the dash branch, stop after the dash, the
  post-dash cast, the cast's (keep, outcome), stop after a cast or Seal of Summer's second cast,
  and the second cast). Each level is a log-softmax in 1/256-nat fixed point with an integer
  log-sum-exp table (`policy_lse.rs`), so priorities are bit-identical in native and wasm. The
  stream pops whole turns best-first by the product of the choice probabilities. The candidate
  SETS inside a cast or a dash are the shipped generator's own; the policy only decides which to
  build and in what order.
* Features are sparse binary (1,247 ids, 41 groups), each weighted by `[1, z]`, with `z` a
  6-vector of position context (lead, turn, castable counts, dash, mana). Spell identity enters
  through the spell-keyed groups. Everything is closed-form from boards the generator already
  holds (no `apply_turn`).
* Training targets: `policy_example` walks the same expansions along a target turn's path, so
  training and serving share one feature code path. `harness/policy_train.py` fits the weights
  on the Step 3 v2 data (searched root candidates, scores as soft targets, unsearched candidates
  unlabeled) and writes `policy_weights.rs`.
* Search switch `set_policy(on, min_width)`: the policy stream is used at nodes whose width
  budget is at least `min_width`; `set_policy_cost(penalty, free_width)` lowers the CONTINUE
  logit (casts and dashes cost generation and `apply_turn` time). Off by default; the shipped
  engine is unchanged (`bench` hash `7bb56101eb4761ad` with the policy off).

## Results

### Node cost: the plan's gate fails

`examples/bench.rs`, 76 midgame positions, one quiet c3d VM (run `s4bench-20261006a`):

| config | depth 4 µs/node | depth 5 µs/node |
|---|---|---|
| shipped (policy off) | 21.6 | 18.3 |
| policy, min_width 0 / 40 / 64 | 56.5 / 55.8 / 54.5 | – / 43.4 / 42.9 |
| policy, min_width 96 | 27.3 | – |
| policy, min_width 160 | 24.7 | – |
| policy 40/64 + continue penalty 512 | 40.1 / 39.3 | – |

`policy_cost`: the policy root costs 6.1 µs, and each expansion costs about 4x the heuristic
stream at the same width (w24: 173 µs vs 41 µs). With the policy at every node, a node costs 2.5x
as much, so the plan's "node rate within 10%" gate **fails**. Gating at min_width 96 uses the
policy only at the wide nodes near the root and costs +26% per node. That still misses the
10% gate.

Mean completed depth at 10 s: 6.83 with the policy against 7.01 without. The policy loses almost
no depth at fixed time despite its per-node cost, because it builds far fewer nodes per ply.

Split by draw (`split_by_draw.py`, spells in ≥150 games): at 10 s with tfit, every spell's
estimate is positive, from +60 (Hail Storm) to +138 (Bewitch). With tfit_spell_v2 the range is
+66 (Seal of Summer) to +171 (Surge). No spell's interval reaches zero. **Expansion draws
(Tectonic/Providence) were not in these arenas.** The default draw is core-only, so a
`SIGIL_REQUIRE_SPELL` arena is still owed before shipping to Providence players.

Reading:
* min_width 96 is the best setting measured. The sweep is not monotone (40: +58, 64: +62,
  96: +99, 128: +67, 160: +58), so 96 sits near a peak, but intervals of about ±21 do not pin
  the peak exactly.
* Lowering width_scale with the policy did not help (3: +87, 2: +89, against +99 at 4).
  Keep the shipped width.
* The gain holds at 10 s (+94) and stacks with the v24 eval: the policy arm with tfit_spell_v2
  beats the shipped search with tfit_spell_v2 by +118. Steps 2 and 4 are independent wins.

### Native / wasm

* The policy compiles into the wasm build. A new off-by-default `set_policy(on, min_width)`
  export (de3f873d) lets `tools/wasm-smoke.js` run with `SIGIL_SMOKE_POLICY=96`. It passes:
  3 games and 197 plies replay-verified, with Tectonic/Providence casts included.
* Size: `sigil_engine_bg.wasm` grows from 738 KB to 841 KB (gzip 240 KB to 282 KB), for the
  compiled weights and code. Smoke wall time: 226 s with the policy against 197 s without.
  That is the same 3 games under timed search, so it is not a speed measure.
* Parity: priorities use fixed-point log-sum-exp tables (`policy_lse.rs`), and the feature
  sums are f32 in a fixed order. No libm calls reach the priority. No test runs the same
  position through native and wasm and compares the streams. Add one, as `evaluate == dot`
  is pinned, before the shipping version bump.

## Recommendation

Ship the policy at **min_width 96, width_scale 4** (policy knob value 96), on top of
tfit_spell_v2, as the next engine version after v24. Before the version bump:
1. Run a 10 s arena with `SIGIL_REQUIRE_SPELL` set to an expansion spell (Tectonic/Providence
   draws).
2. Add a native/wasm parity test of the policy stream.
3. Turn the policy on in `wasm.rs` `configure` (and the worker), rebuild, bump
   RUST_ENGINE_VERSION and the sw.js cache.

The plan's "node rate within 10%" gate is the wrong gate for this change. The policy costs 26%
more per node at min_width 96, and 2.5x at every node, yet it reaches nearly the same depth at
fixed time, sees 2x the surprise replies at 50k nodes, and wins +94 to +118 Elo at 10 s.

Spend: 13.7 VM-hours on c3d-highcpu-90, about $45. That covers the bench, two sees runs, and
eleven arena VMs over both sessions. All `sigil-s4-*` VMs are deleted.
