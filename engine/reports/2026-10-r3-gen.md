Branch `r3-gen` (from `engine-improvement` cd38f524). Knob **`set_policy_explore`** (policy exploration
tail). It is off by default, so the shipped engine is unchanged: the knob-off bench HASH is
`55a10a3b6c3b7ef9`, the v27 hash (nnue_spell3, depth 4).

## 1. Diagnosis (`harness/r3_gen_diag.py`, new `policy_diag` binding)

For each case of `guest_2026-10_cases.json`, the tool takes every exhaustively enumerated turn that
reaches the recorded reply. It walks the policy tree along that turn and reports, at each choice
level, the option count, the target's index and its log-probability. **Index -1 means the CANDIDATE SET
lacks the turn**, so it was pruned before the policy saw it.

Of the 32 cases, 21 are in v27's policy stream (all 14 quiet moves; casts at ranks 0-44). **All 11
that are missing are missing from the candidate sets.** None was ranked too low by the policy:

| case | reply | missing at | detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (00:04 t34) | Starfall dash+cast, MATE | C after dash | keep rank 8 of 10 (keep window 2) |
| 2 (00:23 t25) | Starfall dash+cast, MATE | D | landing rank 10; sacrifice pair beyond the 16 pairs tried per landing |
| 3 (00:30 t23) | Fury cast, MATE | C | keep rank 2 of 3; outcome index 180 |
| 5 (05:46 t25) | Corrupt dash+cast, MATE | C after dash | keep rank 9 of 10 |
| 12 (02:10 t23) | dash, MATE | D | landing rank 0; pair beyond the 16 tried |
| 14 (02:15 t16) | Harvest dash+cast | D | landing 5, pair rank 10 (2 pairs per landing) |
| 15 (02:15 t18) | Scatter cast, MATE | C | keep 1; outside the 16-outcome window |
| 19 (03:09 t15) | Carnage+Slash cast | C | keep rank 3 of 5 |
| 20 (04:45 t31) | **Syzygy dash+cast, MATE** | D | landing rank 13; the 16-branch dash cap holds about 8 landings |
| 26 | dash | D | landing 3, pair rank 4 |
| 28 | Fury dash+cast | C after dash | keep 0, outcome past the window |

The pruning sources are: the cast keep window (2 of up to 10 keeps), the outcome window (16), the dash
branch cap (16 = window), 2 pairs per landing, and 16 pairs tried per landing.

## 2. Fix: the exploration tail (`policy::Explore`, `set_policy_explore`)

When the stream expands a cast stub or a dash stub, it also pushes ONE lazy tail node `base` (1/256 nat)
below the stub's least likely sibling. Popping the tail builds a wider set:
* casts: every keep, `cast_window` outcomes;
* dashes: `dash_limit` branches, `dash_per` pairs per landing, from the `dash_tried` cheapest pairs.

The tail then drops every candidate whose resolved board a sibling already yields (dedupe by outcome).
It pushes the rest `step` apart in the generator's own order.

The tail is never renormalised against the shipped candidates, so the shipped policy stream is an exact
subsequence of the explored one (a test pins this). **Policy input features, training examples
(`policy_example`) and the probabilities of the shipped candidates are unchanged.**

Options that were measured and not kept:
* eval-ordered tails (mode bit 4): same recall, +40-90% generation cost;
* exploration slots (`slot_first`, `slot_every`): no better than the plain tail.

Plumbing: ThreadSwitches (SMP), py `set_policy_explore`, wasm `set_policy_explore`, `bench --explore`,
`policy-wasm-parity.js` `EXPLORE=`, and the `ab_search.py` knob `policy_explore` (presets: 1 =
`3,64,128,8,48,256,8`, 3 = `3,64,128,8,48,512,16`).

## 5. Notes for R3-DATA

* The fix changes **neither the policy's input features nor `policy_example`**. Weights retrained on
  `FEATURE_GROUPS` drop in unchanged.
* With the knob on, the candidate STREAM contains extra tail turns. Their keeps are beyond the keep window,
  their outcomes beyond the outcome window, and their dash landings and pairs beyond the caps.
  `policy_example` still returns target index -1 for such turns. A retrain cannot learn them unless
  the candidate sets themselves are widened.
* `CastOpt` gained an internal `key` field (the resolved board), used for deduplication only.

## 6. Recommendation

Preset 3 (`set_policy_explore(3,64,128,8,48,512,16)`) is the candidate. Before shipping:
1. Finish the 10 s arena to about 1,000+ games per draw type, especially competitive and expansion.
2. Run a wasm 10 s depth check: +17% time to depth 5 natively.

Do not ship on the interim numbers. Only core is significant, and in the browser the cost is paid
single-threaded.
