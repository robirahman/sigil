Core Pack

The foundational terrain of Sigil — five elemental disciplines governing growth, destruction, impact, speed, and deception.

Official Expansion Rated in Competitive Play 15 Spells

Pack Overview & Identity

The Core spell set forms the printed foundation of Sigil. Spanning fifteen spells across five thematic trios (Growth, Havoc, Impact, Tempo, and Hex), the core set establishes the fundamental game dynamics: soft movement expansion, aggressive hard-move pushing, long-range blink strikes, dash mobility, and mind-control conversions. Every standard match draws from this rich pool, balancing raw power with intricate counter-play.
