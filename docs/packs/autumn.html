Pack Overview & Identity

The Autumn expansion contains some of the highest-rated engine tools in the game. Gather and Harvest pump stones directly into your freshly cast, locked spells, creating self-refilling cycles that generate insurmountable stone leads. Meanwhile, Seal of Autumn prevents opponents from sacrificing stones in spells to dash, locking down their mobility.
