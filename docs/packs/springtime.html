Pack Overview & Identity

The Springtime expansion introduces sprawling multi-theater tactics. Rather than contesting one spell at a time, Blossom and Scatter seed friendly stones into multiple 3-node and 5-node sigils simultaneously. Combined with Seal of Spring's ability to cast locked spells a second time, Springtime forces the opponent to defend everywhere at once.
