Pack Overview & Identity

The Celestial expansion turns the board into a cosmic dial. Syzygy strikes straight across the board's opposite ring, while Eclipse and Azimuth reward precise counting by instantly completing spells where you control all but one or two nodes. Celestial rewards calculative players who plan multi-turn conversions and geometric closures.
