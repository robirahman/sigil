Pack Overview & Identity

The Tempest expansion commands the wind to dismantle enemy armies piece by piece. Storm Front provides unmitigated destruction of any two stones anywhere, Hurricane wipes isolated clusters, and Gust physically relocates bordering foes to neutral ground. Tempest disrupts opponent setups with merciless accuracy.
