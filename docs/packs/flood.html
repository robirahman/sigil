Pack Overview & Identity

The Flood expansion introduces chain-reaction resolution. Tsunami and Torrent execute fluid sequences of soft moves followed immediately by hard moves, enabling players to place a stone and push an enemy with the same cast. Splash provides free movement whenever a dash is avoided, rewarding continuous board fluidity.
