About blind mode

Like blindfold chess: the board shows the nodes and spell circles but no stones. Play by clicking (or tabbing to) a node as usual. A panel beside the board reads out every change (“Red on c4; blue off b3”), also to screen readers, and P or its “Describe position” button lists every stone, wall and spell and whose turn it is. Each node also tells a screen reader what is on it. Turn on “Show node names” to see the names on the board, and off “Show legal moves” for a harder game.
