Rules & Kernel Mechanics

The caster targets a spell where exactly two nodes remain uncontrolled (empty or enemy-held) and immediately makes two moves into those nodes, completing or capturing the spell.

Tactical Strategy & Play Advice

Ranked #36 (Bottom tier in general due to strict activation conditions), but acts as an instant finisher for 3-node and 5-node spells that need two final stones to trigger.
