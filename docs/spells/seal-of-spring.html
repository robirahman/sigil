Rules & Kernel Mechanics

Normally, a spell becomes locked upon casting and cannot be cast again until unlocked. While Seal of Spring is active, the caster may cast any of their locked spells once more before requiring an unlock.

Tactical Strategy & Play Advice

Ranked #29 (Weak tier). While situational, Seal of Spring allows double-casting high-impact rituals like Blossom, Carnage, or Corrupt without waiting for lock cycle progression.
