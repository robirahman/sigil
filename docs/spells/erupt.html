Rules & Kernel Mechanics

For every spell on the board (excluding Erupt itself) where the caster already controls at least one stone, the caster makes two moves into that spell.

Ranked #18 (Solid tier). The ultimate wide-board payoff. When paired with Blossom or Scatter, Erupt can generate 6, 8, or 10 moves across the board in a single devastating turn.
