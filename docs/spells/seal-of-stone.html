Rules & Kernel Mechanics

While this seal is active, the opponent is forbidden from leading their turn with a hard move, dash, or push. Their first move each turn must be a soft placement.

Tactical Strategy & Play Advice

Ranked #20 (Middling tier). A powerful defensive dampener that prevents opponent blitz attacks and protects your charging rituals from opening-turn hard move strikes.
