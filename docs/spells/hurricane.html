Rules & Kernel Mechanics

Calculates all contiguous connected groups of enemy stones on the board. The group with the fewest stones is completely destroyed. If there is a tie for smallest size, the caster chooses which group to destroy.

Tactical Strategy & Play Advice

Ranked #24 (Middling tier). Hurricane surgically cleanses isolated enemy scouts, single-node infiltrators, and small clusters. Opponents must keep their stones connected to avoid losing units for free.
