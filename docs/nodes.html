Games, puzzles and the rules name every node of the board. Learn the names: in 30 seconds, find as many nodes as you can, or name them.

How the nodes are named
  • The board has three zones, a, b and c, of 13 nodes each.
  • 1 is the zone's mana node: a1 is where Red starts, b1 where Blue starts, c1 is at the top.
  • 2–6 are the zone's five-node sigil, 7 its charm (one-node sigil) and 8–10 its three-node sigil.
  • 11–13 are the void nodes, between the sigils.
