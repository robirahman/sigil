Variant rules

Variants change how a game starts or how it is won. Pick them under Game options on the main menu, or when you create an online room. Variants combine, except Deathmatch with Scramble. Two official packs also bring rules of their own.

These rules are what the game's rules engine plays. For the basics, play the tutorial; for each spell, see its rules page.
